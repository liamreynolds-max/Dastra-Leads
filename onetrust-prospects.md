## Why Now: The OneTrust Disruption Window (2025–2026)

Three simultaneous disruptions make Q1–Q3 2026 the optimal window to engage confirmed OneTrust accounts:

1. **PE acquisition rumours** (November 2025): *The Information* reported OneTrust exploring a sale to private equity at a $4.5–10B+ valuation. PE ownership historically triggers pricing optimisation and product rationalisation. Customers are being advised to negotiate protections and evaluate alternatives at their next renewal.

2. **Aggressive 2025 price hikes**: G2 reviews document price increases of 275–468% with as little as 21 days' notice. UK charities facing 10x renewal increases (from <£1,000 to >£17,000/year). One buyer reported a ~59.1% uplift proposed on a flat renewal.

3. **Training abandonment**: A Capterra UK reviewer noted OneTrust "will not be updating their training materials anymore, at least not for the UK market" — a signal of reduced commitment to the UK mid-market.

**Dastra's competitive positioning**: EU-built, transparent pricing, modular, simpler UX, with no US PE overhang. These disruptions create a credible and timely switching narrative.

## Market Intelligence: The OneTrust Switching Window

### Key Triggers to Reference in Outreach

1. **PE acquisition (Nov 2025)**: "Following reports of a private equity sale — at a rumoured $10B+ valuation — many customers are proactively evaluating whether OneTrust remains their long-term partner. Can we show you what Dastra looks like?"

2. **Pricing shock**: "We're hearing from organisations receiving renewal notices with 200–400% increases. If that's happening for you too, the conversation is worth having before your renewal locks in."

3. **UK training withdrawal**: "OneTrust has reportedly stopped updating its training materials for the UK market. For teams building or maturing their privacy programme, that's a meaningful gap — one Dastra closes with dedicated UK/EU customer success."

4. **EU hosting**: "OneTrust processes your data through US infrastructure under a US-owned (soon PE-owned) entity. Dastra was built in France, is hosted in the EU, and was designed for GDPR-first organisations."

### Competitive Displacement Playbook

**Step 1 — Trigger the conversation**: Reference the OneTrust PE news or pricing disruption. Do not disparage OneTrust — frame it as a "natural review moment."

**Step 2 — Module-level comparison**: Map their confirmed OneTrust modules to Dastra equivalents. Vanquis → RoPA + DPIA + Vendor Assessment + DSAR. INTO → DPIA + Data Mapping. Samaritans → CMP/Consent.

**Step 3 — Total cost of ownership**: OneTrust median customer pays $11,500/year; enterprise customers $42,500+. Position Dastra's pricing transparency as a structural advantage, not just a price point.

**Step 4 — Migration assistance**: Offer data migration from OneTrust exports (RoPA records, assessment templates, vendor inventories). Reduce switching cost to zero.

**Step 5 — Close on renewal timing**: OneTrust typically locks customers into 12–24 month contracts. Target accounts 3–4 months before their renewal window.

## Gaps and Research Notes

- **Privacy portal URL detection**: A more systematic audit of `.onetrust.com` subdomain URLs in UK company privacy policies would surface dozens of additional confirmed users beyond those in OneTrust's own case studies. This is achievable via a automated crawl or manual review of FTSE 350 and ISEQ 20 company privacy policy pages.
- **G-Cloud procurement**: OneTrust is listed on the UK Government Digital Marketplace. Public sector organisations that have purchased OneTrust via G-Cloud are theoretically searchable via Contracts Finder, though OneTrust frequently contracts directly. A Contracts Finder search for "OneTrust" or "privacy management platform" procurement awards would be a productive next step.
- **Samaritans referral route**: Torchbox (the charity digital agency leading the campaign against OneTrust pricing) may be willing to introduce Dastra to affected nonprofit clients. A partnership or referral conversation with Torchbox's Chief Strategy Officer Phil McMinn is recommended.
- **Irish market**: No confirmed Irish-HQ OneTrust case studies were found. The DPC's enforcement environment makes this surprising — a targeted search of Irish company privacy policy pages for OneTrust consent banner signatures (the `OneTrustActiveGroups` cookie or `onetrust-accept-btn-handler` class) would identify additional prospects.
